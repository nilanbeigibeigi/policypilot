# Group Benefits Booklet — Ridgeline Contracting Ltd.

> Fictional client company and plan. All numbers are synthetic sample data, not a real insurance contract.

## Eligibility
Full-time employees working at least 25 hours per week are eligible. Coverage starts on the first day of the month after 3 months of continuous employment. Spouses and dependent children are covered under family coverage. A son or daughter is covered to age 21, or to age 25 if they are a full-time student.

## Extended Health Care
The plan pays 80% of eligible prescription drugs with a pay-direct drug card. There is no annual deductible. Vision care pays up to $250 every 24 months for glasses or contact lenses, plus one eye exam every 24 months.

## Paramedical Services
Massage therapy, physiotherapy, chiropractic and registered psychologist visits are each covered at 80%, up to $500 per practitioner type per calendar year. A doctor's referral is not required.

## Dental Care
Basic dental services such as cleanings, fillings and x-rays are covered at 80%. Major dental services such as crowns and bridges are covered at 50%, up to $1,500 per person per calendar year. Recall exams are covered once every 9 months. Orthodontics are not covered.

## Life and Disability Insurance
Basic life insurance is 2 times annual salary, to a maximum of $200,000. Long-term disability pays 66.7% of monthly earnings to a maximum of $5,000 per month, after a waiting period of 119 days.

## Making a Claim
Claims must be submitted within 12 months of the date of service. Claims can be submitted through the insurer's mobile app or by mail. Most electronic claims are paid by direct deposit within 5 business days.

## Leaving the Company
Coverage ends on the last day of employment. Employees can convert their basic life insurance to an individual policy without medical evidence if they apply within 31 days of leaving.
