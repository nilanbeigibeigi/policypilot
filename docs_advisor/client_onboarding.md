# Client Onboarding Procedure — Brightwater Wealth Partners

> Fictional firm. All names, numbers and rules in this document are synthetic sample data.

## Discovery Meeting
Every new client starts with a free 60-minute discovery meeting, in person or by video call. The advisor explains how the firm is paid, collects the client's goals, and books the planning meeting within 14 days. No product is recommended at the discovery meeting.

## Documents Required
Before the planning meeting the client uploads: two pieces of government photo ID, the last two years of Notices of Assessment, recent statements for every investment account, all existing insurance policies, and a void cheque. Business owners also provide the last two years of corporate financial statements.

## Know Your Client Form
The Know Your Client (KYC) form must be completed and signed before any account is opened. It records income, net worth, investment knowledge, time horizon and risk tolerance. KYC information must be updated at least every 36 months, or within 30 days of a major life event such as marriage, divorce, a new child, retirement or the sale of a business.

## Risk Profile
Each client is placed in one of five risk profiles: Conservative, Moderately Conservative, Balanced, Growth, or Aggressive Growth. The profile is set by the advisor using the risk questionnaire score and the client's time horizon. A client with a time horizon under 3 years cannot be placed above Balanced.

## Account Opening Timeline
Accounts are normally opened within 5 business days after the signed KYC form and all documents are received. Transfers from other institutions usually take 2 to 4 weeks. The office administrator emails the client a status update every Friday until all transfers are complete.
