# Life Insurance Policy Summary — Sample Client File (Term 20)

> Fictional client and policy. All numbers are synthetic sample data, not a real insurance contract.

## Policy Overview
The insured is the client, age 38 at issue. The policy is a 20-year term life policy with a death benefit of $750,000. The monthly premium is $62.40 and is guaranteed not to change for the 20-year term.

## Beneficiaries
The primary beneficiary is the client's spouse at 100%. The contingent beneficiaries are the client's two children in equal shares, with a trustee named because they are minors. Beneficiaries can be changed at any time by signing a change-of-beneficiary form, unless a beneficiary has been named irrevocably.

## Conversion Option
The policy can be converted to a permanent life policy without medical evidence at any time before the insured's 65th birthday or before the end of the term, whichever comes first.

## Renewal
At the end of the 20-year term the policy renews automatically for another term at a much higher premium based on the insured's age at renewal. The renewal premium schedule is in the contract.

## Grace Period and Lapse
If the client misses a premium payment, there is a 31-day grace period during which coverage stays in force. If the premium is still unpaid after the grace period, the policy lapses. A lapsed policy can be reinstated within 2 years with proof of good health and payment of missed premiums.

## Exclusions
Suicide within the first 2 years of the policy is excluded; in that case only premiums paid are refunded. The policy is contestable for misrepresentation for the first 2 years.
