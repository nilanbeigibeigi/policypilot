# Complaints and Compliance — Brightwater Wealth Partners

> Fictional firm. All names, numbers and rules in this document are synthetic sample data.

## Receiving a Complaint
Any client complaint, verbal or written, is logged in the complaint register the same day it is received. The client receives a written acknowledgement within 5 business days.

## Resolving a Complaint
The firm aims to send a final written response within 90 days. If the client is not satisfied with the final response, they are told in writing about the independent ombudservice they can contact.

## Trade Errors
A trade placed in error must be reported to the principal advisor immediately and corrected the same business day if possible. The client must never be left worse off because of a firm error.

## Gifts and Entertainment
Staff may not accept gifts from clients worth more than $100 per year. All gifts over $25 are recorded in the gifts register.

## Outside Activities
Any outside business activity, including part-time work, volunteer board roles or a side business, must be disclosed to the principal advisor and approved before it starts.
