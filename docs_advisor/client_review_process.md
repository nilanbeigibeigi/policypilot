# Client Review Process — Brightwater Wealth Partners

> Fictional firm. All names, numbers and rules in this document are synthetic sample data.

## Review Frequency
Clients with more than $500,000 in assets get two reviews per year. All other clients get one annual review. Group benefits clients get one plan review per year, 60 days before the renewal date.

## Preparing for a Review
Two weeks before a review, the administrator prepares the review package: current account values, performance since the last review, the KYC form on file, insurance coverage summary, and any open action items from the last meeting. The advisor reviews the package at least 2 business days before the meeting.

## Life Event Triggers
An off-cycle review is booked within 30 days when a client reports marriage, divorce, a new child, a death in the family, a job change, an inheritance over $50,000, or the purchase or sale of a business or home.

## Meeting Notes
Meeting notes must be saved in the client file within 2 business days. Notes must include the topics discussed, any recommendations, the client's decision, and next steps with owners and dates. Notes are never shared outside the firm without the client's written consent.

## Follow-Up
Every review ends with a follow-up email sent within 3 business days summarizing decisions and next steps. Action items that are not completed within 30 days are escalated to the advisor.
