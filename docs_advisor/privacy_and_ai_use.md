# Privacy, Data and AI Use Policy — Brightwater Wealth Partners

> Fictional firm. All names, numbers and rules in this document are synthetic sample data.

## Client Information
Client personal and financial information is stored only in the firm's approved client management system and the dealer's secure portal. Client files are never saved on personal devices or personal cloud accounts.

## Sending Documents
Documents containing a client's SIN, account numbers or statements are sent only through the secure client portal, never by regular email. Phone requests to change banking details must be confirmed by calling the client back at the number on file.

## Use of AI Tools
Staff may use only AI tools approved by the principal advisor. Client names, SINs, account numbers and statements must never be pasted into public AI chatbots, for example to summarize or rewrite a client statement. Approved AI tools must keep data in Canada or run on the firm's own computers, and must not train on firm data. Any AI-generated client communication must be reviewed by an advisor before it is sent.

## Record Retention
Client records are kept for at least 7 years after the end of the client relationship. Paper documents are scanned into the client file within 5 business days and then shredded with the office shredding service.

## Privacy Breach
Any suspected privacy breach, such as an email sent to the wrong client, must be reported to the principal advisor the same day. The principal advisor decides within 48 hours whether clients and regulators must be notified.
