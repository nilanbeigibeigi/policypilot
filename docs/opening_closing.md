# Opening and Closing Procedures — Saffron Market

## Opening Checklist
The opening lead arrives 45 minutes before the store opens at 9:00 AM. Disarm the alarm within 30 seconds of entering. Check fridge and freezer temperatures and record them in the temperature log. Count the opening cash float of $200 per till. Turn on the bakery oven by 8:30 AM so fresh bread is ready at opening.

## Closing Checklist
The store closes at 9:00 PM. Lock the front doors at 9:00 PM exactly and let remaining customers finish. Pull expired and damaged products and record them in the waste log. Clean the deli slicer and bakery counters. Record final fridge and freezer temperatures.

## Cash Handling
Two employees must be present when counting the tills at closing. Each till is counted down to the $200 float, and the remainder goes into a sealed deposit bag. A till that is over or short by more than $10 must be reported to the store manager the same night.

## Alarm and Lock-Up
The closing lead sets the alarm and checks that the back door is locked. Never leave the store alone after closing — at least two employees leave together. The alarm code changes every 3 months and is shared by the store manager only.
