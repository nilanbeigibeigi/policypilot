# Workplace Safety and Incidents — Saffron Market

## Spills
Place a wet-floor sign immediately, then clean the spill. Never leave a spill unattended, even for a moment.

## Injuries
Any employee or customer injury, however small, must be reported to the manager right away. Complete the incident report form within 24 hours. The first-aid kit is in the staff room, next to the fire extinguisher.

## Robbery
Stay calm and do not resist. Hand over cash if asked; your safety is more important than money. Call 911 once it is safe and do not touch anything the robber touched.

## Fire and Evacuation
The meeting point is the parking lot light pole near the entrance. The shift lead does a headcount at the meeting point. Fire drills are held twice a year.
