# Food Safety Rules — Saffron Market

## Temperatures
Fridges must stay at 4°C or below. Freezers must stay at −18°C or below. Hot food in the deli must be held at 60°C or above. Check and log temperatures at opening, 2:00 PM, and closing. If a fridge is above 4°C for more than 2 hours, move the food to another fridge and tell the manager.

## Date Labels
Every prepared deli item must be labelled with the product name and the date and time it was made. Prepared salads and dips are sold for a maximum of 3 days. Rotate stock first-in, first-out: older products go to the front.

## Allergens
Pistachio, walnut, almond, sesame, and dairy are the main allergens in our products. Use separate scoops and cutting boards for nut products. If a customer asks about allergens, show them the ingredient label; never guess.

## Handwashing
Wash hands for at least 20 seconds before handling food, after breaks, after handling raw meat, and after touching money. Gloves are required in the deli and butcher areas and must be changed between tasks.

## Certification
All deli and kitchen staff must hold a valid FOODSAFE Level 1 certificate within 30 days of being hired.
