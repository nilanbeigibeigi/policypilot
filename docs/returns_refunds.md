# Returns and Refunds Policy — Saffron Market

## Standard Returns
Unopened, non-perishable products can be returned within 30 days with a receipt for a full refund to the original payment method. Without a receipt, customers can receive store credit only.

## Perishable Products
Fresh produce, bakery, deli, dairy, and meat can be returned within 48 hours if the product was spoiled or unsafe when purchased. Perishable returns are refunded or exchanged, and the product must be recorded in the waste log.

## Items That Cannot Be Returned
Opened spices and saffron, gift cards, and items bought on final clearance cannot be returned.

## Manager Approval
Any refund over $100, or any refund without a receipt, requires manager approval. Cashiers may approve refunds of $100 or less with a receipt.
