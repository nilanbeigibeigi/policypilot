# Loyalty Program — Saffron Rewards

## Earning Points
Members earn 1 point for every $1 spent, before tax. Points are not earned on gift card purchases.

## Redeeming Points
Every 100 points can be redeemed for $5 off a purchase. Points can be redeemed at any till by scanning the member's card or phone number.

## Tiers
Silver is the starting tier. Members who spend $1,000 in a calendar year become Gold and earn 1.5 points per $1. Members who spend $3,000 become Platinum and earn 2 points per $1 plus a free gift box before Nowruz.

## Point Expiry
Points expire 12 months after they are earned. Members can sign up for free at any till.
