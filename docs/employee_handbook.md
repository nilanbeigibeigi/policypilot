# Employee Handbook — Saffron Market

## Shifts and Scheduling
Schedules are posted every Thursday for the following week in the staff room and in the scheduling app. Shift swaps must be approved by a manager at least 48 hours before the shift starts. Employees must clock in no earlier than 5 minutes before their scheduled start time.

## Breaks
Employees working more than 5 hours get one unpaid 30-minute meal break. Shifts of 8 hours also include two paid 10-minute rest breaks. Breaks cannot be skipped to leave early.

## Sick Days
Full-time and part-time employees receive 5 paid sick days per calendar year after 90 days of employment. Call your manager at least 2 hours before your shift if you are sick. A doctor's note is only required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Vacation
Employees earn 2 weeks of paid vacation per year, increasing to 3 weeks after 5 years of service. Vacation requests must be submitted at least 3 weeks in advance. No vacation is approved during the two weeks before Nowruz or the week before Yalda.

## Dress Code
Wear the Saffron Market apron and name tag on every shift. Closed-toe, non-slip shoes are mandatory. Long hair must be tied back in food areas.

## Employee Discount
Employees receive a 15% discount on regular-priced items. The discount does not apply to sale items, gift cards, or saffron over 5 grams. The discount is for the employee's household only.
